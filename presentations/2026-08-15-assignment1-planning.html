Criterion 1 · Problem statement · 4 points

Our problem statement names cells, not adjectives

  • Every team will write “spreadsheets are fragile”. We can cite the cells that are wrong today.
  • A $2,079.08 platform “surplus” that is entirely an artefact of one wrong reference.
  • Platform revenue understated by $4,874.74 — cost sums eight columns, revenue six.
  • A server-side engine with a fixed aggregate contract makes each defect structurally impossible, not merely unlikely.
  • Evidence first, then the design that follows from it. That is the spine of the whole submission.
