Underneath every screen

What the general backend guarantees

  • Sealed means sealed. Approval writes inputs, results, workings and every explanation into one snapshot, with a SHA-256 fingerprint.
  • Old records keep opening. The record format is versioned; earlier records still open exactly as sealed.
  • One door to every record. Every step loads its cycle through one class that applies the ownership check.
  • Mistypes stop at the server. Text for a number, a percentage over 100 or an extra zero needs attention first.
  • Explanations are enforced. A varied rate, a deficit or missing utilisation assumptions block submission.

Low frequency, high stakes

Made for a job done once every three to five years

  • The step bar is navigation. Every finished step is a link back; nothing links forward to figures that are not yet saved.
  • Nothing typed is lost. Going back saves first, and leaving a half-typed entry asks before throwing it away.
  • Messages in a custodian’s words. “Enter a number, such as 20000.00” — and a refusal sits where the number would be, never a plausible $0.00.
  • Meaning never rests on colour. A deficit is written as a word and in brackets; a variance says “above” or “below”.
  • Accessible on any width. The current step is announced to screen readers; on a narrow screen the step names fold away but stay readable to them.

Why the numbers can be trusted in 2030

An engine that follows your guide

  • The guide governs. The commercial rate deducts no income, as you confirmed in writing on 20 August.
  • Exact decimal arithmetic, rounded once on the way to the screen or the PDF, so a total never drifts from its parts.
  • It refuses rather than guesses. With no forecast use it says what is missing — it never shows $0.00.
  • The method is versioned. A record sealed today still recalculates under version 2026.1 in 2030, whatever the factor is by then.
  • Capacity is built, not typed: the machine (1,882.5 h) or staff (1,725 h) baseline, less deductions, each with a note.
  • The balance two ways — against cost less income, and against full economic cost — with overheads on their own line.

Chenxu You

What the layer is built on

Six principles behind every sign-in

Deny by default

Every page needs sign-in and every area its role, checked on the server at each request — not hidden in a menu.

Nothing to guess with

Five failed attempts lock an account for 15 minutes. A wrong username and a wrong password get the same reply.

Least privilege

Each role sees only what its work needs. An administrator can look at any record, but never act in someone else’s name.

Sessions that can be ended

A two-hour, HTTP-only session. A password change, reset or deactivation ends every other session at once.

No home-made cryptography

Passwords are salted and hashed by the framework — PBKDF2 at 210,000 iterations — and re-hashed as the standard rises.

Safe on a real server

No demo account exists outside development. Accounts are deactivated, never deleted, so names on sealed records stay true.
