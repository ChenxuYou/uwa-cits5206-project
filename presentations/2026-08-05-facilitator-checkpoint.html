What We Learned From the Client

Chenxu You

Section 1 of 5

1 · Learned from the clientChenxu You

One recorded walkthrough, 29 July

UWA must price its research platforms consistently — and prove why

The setting

  • Electron microscopes, a human MRI, radio telescopes, phenotyping drones — expensive to buy and to run
  • Publicly funded, so costing must be transparent and identical in method across every platform
  • The goal is break-even, not profit and not replacement cost

Who actually uses the tool

  • Platform leaders — UWA academic or professional staff who run a facility, not the researchers who buy time
  • Busy technical people; this is admin work they tolerate rather than enjoy
  • Run once every 3–5 years — low frequency, high stakes

Source: client meeting transcript, 29 July 2026.

1 · Learned from the clientChenxu You

The sentence that defines the product

If someone comes to us and says ‘why does it cost $50 an hour for me to use?’, we want to be able to say: well, it costs $100,000 a year to run, this is how many hours a year it’s going to be used, we divide one by the other — $50 an hour. That’s the reason we charge that price. UWA Research Infrastructure · 29 July 2026

Everything else — the wizard, the validation, the sealed record — exists to make that answer available three years after the decision was made.

Our Understanding of the Requirements

2 · RequirementsChenxu You

The calculation, as we understand it

Costs, minus subsidies, divided by forecast use

R_internal   = (C − I_uwa − I_gov) / U
R_national   = ((C − I_gov) / U) × k
R_commercial = (C / U) × k

C total operating cost · I non-variable income · U forecast utilisation · k = 1.35

  • Costs are captured per instrument and per platform — the demo facility held seven instruments
  • Forecast utilisation, not capacity, is the divisor. 1,000 h capacity may be 500 h of real use
  • k = 1.35 is UWA’s standard indirect cost recovery for any external party — insurance, legal, finance, library, buildings, IT

We will verify this against the client’s own spreadsheet as a golden-file test before writing any UI.

Cadence and collaboration

Weekly online, fortnightly in person, everything in the repository

Meeting rhythm

  • Weekly team stand-up online — progress, blockers, next week’s allocation
  • Fortnightly face-to-face on campus, including this facilitator checkpoint
  • Client on Wednesdays as needed, plus a shared Teams chat for async questions

How we work together

  • GitHub Projects board; every task an issue with one named owner and a deadline
  • Markdown and plain text only, so every change diffs and review is visible
  • Minutes recorded and committed within 24 hours — two members missed the client walkthrough and were briefed from them

Cadence agreed with the client on 29 July: heavier support up front, easing off later.

Professional obligations

Two codes govern how we behave on this project

ACS Code of Professional Ethics (2023)

Honesty · Trustworthiness · Respect

In practice: we do not overstate what we have built, we declare what is still an assumption, and we take responsibility for failures as well as successes.

UWA Code of Conduct

Behave honestly and with integrity · act with care and diligence · treat everyone with respect and courtesy · comply with applicable laws · uphold the University’s reputation.

The Code’s own test: would I be happy for this to appear on the front page of the newspaper?

We are building a tool whose entire purpose is public accountability for public money. Holding ourselves to the same standard is not decoration.

3 · Ways of workingChenxu You

Generative AI

Used deliberately, declared openly, never as a substitute for our thinking

What we do

  • Follow the AI use tier and AI use statement set for each assessment — the assessment brief is definitive, not the general guide
  • Keep a log of tools, prompts and outputs, and submit an acknowledgement statement
  • Check every AI output ourselves — we remain responsible for accuracy

What we never do

  • Put client data, personal data or third-party IP into a public AI tool — the client’s spreadsheet has not yet been cleared for this
  • Use AI to replace critical thinking or analysis
  • Use DeepSeek — prohibited on all UWA networks and devices

Source: Using Artificial Intelligence Tools at UWA: A Guide for Students, updated March 2026 · UWA Academic Integrity Policy. Open  We have asked the client whether AI tools may be used on their material; not yet answered.

What the Architecture Will Look Like

Yichen Zhao

Section 4 of 5

4 · ArchitectureYichen Zhao

Three tiers, one protected engine

The calculation logic lives where the user cannot reach it

The engine is a pure module with no database or UI dependency: decimal arithmetic, a divide-by-zero guard, and a versioned config so a 2026 record still reproduces its figures in 2030.
