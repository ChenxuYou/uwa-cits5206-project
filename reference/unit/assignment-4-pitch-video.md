## 2. The brief, as set

### Preparation

> Each individual will create a video presentation (screen capture or talking to camera) of the
> project. The project video will be no more than 5 minutes long (Your mark for the project pitch
> will be deducted, if your video is too long). The video will motivate the overall project,
> demonstrate the software features/contributions you made, and discuss what future plans could
> be. The project video should be pitched for the project client and other stakeholders. Each
> team member should discuss the whole project (2 minutes) and their contributions (3 minutes)
> in their video.
>
> Please show your face in your presentation. You will also need to use your own voice to
> explain the best features of your App, no AI voices or faces accepted.

### Submission

> Upload your video to a platform, such as YouTube and OneDrive.
>
> Then paste your links into the text area of the Create Submission field for this submission.
>
> Ensure your link can be accessed by markers (test by your friends please for the link; pay
> attention to the expiry date).
>
> Only the link in the Create Submission field will be used for marking.
>
> Do not attempt to Upload video files to LMS or use the Add Comments Field. Any information in
> those field will not be marked.
>
> Students might be selected to ask questions regarding their contributions.

## 4. Reading the brief and rubric

Our reading, not the unit's words.

| # | What the brief and rubric say | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R1** | **Time is all or nothing, and it costs twice.** *Time* is 1 point for ≤ 5:00 and 0 otherwise, and the brief says the pitch mark will also be deducted if the video is too long | Aim for about **4:30** so there is margin. Check the length the platform reports after upload, not the editor's timeline |
| **R2** | **Your own work is a third of the points shown.** *Individual Contributions* (2) and *Software features contributed* (2) are 4 of the 12 points, and the brief gives them the 3-minute segment | Spend the 3 minutes on what **you** built, shown on screen. The *Skilled* band for 3.6 turns on it being clear "what parts are written by you" — name the features, show them running, and say which parts are yours |
| **R3** | **The 2-minute project segment carries four criteria.** *Requirements/needs*, *Proposal*, *Demonstration* and *Future Plan* are 1 point each | In about 2 minutes: the problem, the goal, a short demo of the app as a whole, then (at the end) next steps. Keep each tight |
| **R4** | **The need wants evidence.** The *Skilled* band for 3.2 asks for "evidence or data to support its significance" | Use a concrete figure or fact from the client brief or meetings, not just "the client needs a tool" |
| **R5** | **The future plan must be concrete.** *Skilled* for 3.7 asks for "realistic steps and goals that build on the project's current achievements" | Name specific next steps that follow from what exists now, not a wish list |
| **R6** | **It is pitched to the client and stakeholders**, not to the markers | Explain benefits in the client's terms; keep jargon and code walkthroughs to what supports the contribution claims |
| **R7** | **Delivery and production are 3 points.** *Fluency*, *Organisation and logic*, *Audio and Visual Quality* | Script or outline it, rehearse, use a decent microphone in a quiet room, and record the screen at a resolution where text is readable |
| **R8** | **Hard requirements outside the rubric.** Face shown, own voice, no AI voices or faces | Keep your face visible (e.g. a webcam overlay during screen capture). These are conditions of the brief, not rubric points — breaking them may put the whole submission at risk |
| **R9** | **Only the link is marked**, and it must work for markers | Set sharing to "anyone with the link" (YouTube *Unlisted* works), turn off any **expiry date** (OneDrive links can have one), and have a friend open it signed out or from another account before submitting |
| **R10** | **You may be questioned on your contributions** | Only claim work you can explain in detail; the D2 software feature report is the record to stay consistent with |
