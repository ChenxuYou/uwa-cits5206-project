# Assignment 1 — Marking Rubric

Total: 15 points

| Criterion | Points | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem Statement: What are you going to build and why | 4 | 26.67% |
| Client communication and MVP agreement | 4 | 26.67% |
| Project Management and Plans | 4 | 26.67% |
| Risk and Technology Assessments | 3 | 20% |

---

## 1. Problem Statement: What are you going to build and why

**4 possible points (26.67%)**

| Level | Score | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developing | 0 – 1 | Unclear or vague problem statement and MVP. |
| Competent | 2 | Some statements about the problem to be addressed, why the client wants this software. MVP is not clearly articulated. |
| Exceptional | 3 – 4 | Clear statement of the problem to be addressed, why the client wants this software, and key deliverables for the MVP. |

## 2. Client communication and MVP agreement

**4 possible points (26.67%)**

| Level | Score | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developing | 0 – 1 | Unclear or vague evidence of communication and project outcomes. |
| Competent | 2 | Some communication with client; MVP not clearly articulated or agreed; lacks evidence of good outcomes from this. |
| Exceptional | 3 – 4 | Evidence of good communications with the client and other stakeholders; client has approved MVP and any other deliverables to date. |

## 3. Project Management and Plans

**4 possible points (26.67%)**

| Level | Score | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developing | 0 – 1 | Insufficient evidence of planning for the next project stage. |
| Competent | 2 | Plan is unrealistic in some aspects or has insufficient detail for the next stages of the project. Limited evidence of effective use of tools for project management. |
| Exceptional | 3 – 4 | Project plan is realistic and contains sufficient detail for each team member to work on the next deliverable. Responsibilities and deadlines are documented. Project tools are set up and show evidence of effective planning for group workflow and software deployment. |

## 4. Risk and Technology Assessments

**3 possible points (20%)**

| Level | Score | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developing | 0 – 1 | Little evidence of systematic evaluation of risks, skills or technology choices for the project. |
| Competent | 2 | Some evidence of risks, skills and technology assessment; limited evidence of effective evaluation or justification of choices. |
| Exceptional | 3 | The team has made a realistic assessment of their skills, resources and risks for this project. Skills gaps have been identified and addressed. The team has carefully considered different choices of technology for the project and clearly justified the decisions made. Relevant risks, including cybersecurity, have been identified and planned for. |
