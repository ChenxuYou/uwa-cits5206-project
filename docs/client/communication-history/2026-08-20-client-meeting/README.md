## Which document governs

**The signed PDF and the client's written answers are the evidence.** The meeting notes are
ours, written while someone was talking, and they are useful for intent and unreliable for
wording. Where the notes and the written answers differ, the written answers win, and the
difference is stated in the notes rather than quietly reconciled.

## A note on committing these files

The two PDFs are **our own documents** returned to us — the scope statement we wrote, signed,
and the question sheet we wrote, annotated by us. They are not client material within the
meaning of [`.gitignore`](../../../../.gitignore) §1, which covers documents the client hands us.

They carry a name, a role and a signature date. That is the point of them: criterion 2 of the
rubric turns on the client having approved the MVP, and a sign-off with the signatory removed
proves nothing. Both are committed deliberately, and the markdown transcriptions sit beside them
so that the content diffs in GitHub even though the PDFs do not.
