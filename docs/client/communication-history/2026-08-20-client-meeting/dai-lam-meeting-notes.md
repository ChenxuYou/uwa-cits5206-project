## Question 1 — who may see and approve a record

Highlighted: *"delegated authority (sees what's submitted to them)"*, and *"If UWA single
sign-on is needed"*.

> the application will need a nominated person to approve, which will be granted by the
> administrator

> link with the HR system to get the corresponding roles of staffs

**Reading.** Two separate things came out of this question, and only one of them is settled.

1. **Approval.** The client wants a *named* approver on each record, and the administrator is
   who nominates them. The written answer condenses this to "administrator is approver of the
   record". Both readings agree on the administrator's authority; neither says approval has to
   be a routed in-tool step in the MVP. **F16 stays Should** until the client says otherwise,
   and we say so in the minutes rather than assume it either way.
2. **Identity from HR.** Pulling staff roles from a UWA HR system is *new*, and it is exactly
   the class of work our scope statement defers (`integration with UWA finance, HR or booking
   systems`). It is recorded as raised, not accepted — see the
   [minutes](../../../meetings/2026-08-20-client-meeting.md) §5.

## Question 2 — multi-year cycles

> no amendment needed, if things happen badly, they will open the problem and submit a new one
> in the next year. just keep the old records.

**Reading.** Matches the written answer and matches our default. Supersession only; no
amendment chain. Q3 closes.

## Question 3 — the sealed record

Highlighted: *"PDF"*.

> no template needed, just require some summary of diff sections

**Reading.** PDF confirmed, no UWA template to build to. The note about "summary of diff
sections" is our shorthand for what the written answer states more precisely: the PDF should
carry **the workings** of the calculation as well as the outputs. The written answer also adds
what the note does not — the filing destination, **Content Manager (TRIM)**.

## Question 4 — commercial rates, guide or calculator

No annotation. Answered in writing: **the guide governs**, and discrepancies are to be flagged
to the client.

## Question 5 — guide or calculator generally

> maybe should propose versions for the clients to review and distinguish the diff

**Reading.** This is ours, not the client's — a suggestion made in the room that where our
figure differs from the calculator's, we show both and let the client see the difference. It
sits well beside the client's written request that discrepancies be **flagged to them**, and the
two together are why N14 and the show-both rule stay in the design.
