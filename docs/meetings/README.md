## Conventions

- **File name:** `YYYY-MM-DD-<type>-meeting.md`, where `<type>` is `client`, `facilitator` or
  `team` and matches the folder. The topic goes in the title inside the file, not in the name.
- **Header:** date with weekday, format, who was present, and the purpose in one sentence.
  Anything not recorded at the time is marked _to confirm_, never guessed.
- **Body:** what was discussed, then *Decisions* and *Actions* tables. Actions carry an owner and
  a date.
- **When:** committed within 24 hours of the meeting ([`plan.md` §4](../project/plan.md)). A
  record written up later says so in an *About this record* note.
- **After the fact:** a meeting record is not rewritten later. Corrections go in a dated note, and
  only links are updated when files move.
- **Recordings and transcripts** (`.m4a`, `.srt`, `.vtt`, `*-transcript.md`) sit beside their
  minutes on your own machine, with the same base name, and are never committed
  ([`.gitignore`](../../.gitignore) §3).
- **Decks** presented at a meeting live in [`presentations/`](../../presentations/) under the same
  date.
